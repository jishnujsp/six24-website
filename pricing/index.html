Billing questions

Good to know

What is the difference between yearly and monthly billing?

Yearly billing costs less and is paid upfront for the year. Monthly billing costs a little more and has no lock-in.

How is the headcount counted?

Each plan includes a set number of employees, and every active employee above that is billed per employee. Consultants and interns count as employees.

Do candidates or people who have left cost anything?

No. Candidates are free until they join. People who have left become free once their full-and-final settlement is done, and they keep free access to their payslips and Form 16.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 7 days on any plan. There is no free plan.

Who sets it up?

We do: your data, payroll, policies and training. You don't have to plan a migration. You just use the product.

Is WhatsApp included?

Yes, on every plan, up to 100 messages per employee per month, at no extra cost.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. If you need a second legal company on Six, or two of Perform, Desk and Comply on Twelve, moving up a plan usually costs less than adding them separately.
