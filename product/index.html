People Ops

Everything waiting on HR, on one screen

Quick View opens with what needs you today. The People Ops dashboard covers headcount, attrition, tenure and locations across all your companies.

  • Quick View: pending approvals, open tickets, who is away or remote, recent joiners
  • Workforce rhythm: who is working, in the office or remote over the last 14 working days
  • Dashboard: headcount by department, worker type, gender, age bands, tenure and location
  • HR tools: CTC, notice and end-date, gratuity and leave encashment calculators
Hiring

From requisition to accepted offer

Jobs, candidates, interviews and offers in one place, with the approvals your company needs and none it doesn't.

  • Requisitions and budget approval: switch on only if you need them
  • AI screening you confirm: it recommends, a person decides, nothing moves on its own
  • Interviews: scheduled with calendar invites for Google, Outlook and Zoho
  • Offers: approval before release, then e-sign or physical signature
  • Agencies and careers page: a private portal per agency and a public page for open jobs
  • True cost per hire: agency fees and referral bonuses counted in
Onboarding

Every joiner, from offer to first day

Accepted offers move to Onboarding on their own. Each joiner follows the same steps, and nobody is added as an employee until their first day is approved.

  • Pipeline: salary approved, offer letter, agreement, added to the system, onboarding steps
  • Candidate portal: documents, welcome note and first-week plan before day one
  • Document reading: details read from uploaded documents, so HR checks instead of typing
  • Probation: reviews, extensions and confirmation letters by policy
Leave & Attendance

Requests that reach a decision

Leave, work from home and on-duty requests with a clear approval path and a record of who decided what.

  • Manager, then HR: requests waiting two days with a manager move to HR
  • Missing punches: flagged the same afternoon, not at month end
  • Balances and holidays: per policy, per company and per location
  • Team view: who is out, remote or on duty today
Finance & Payroll

Fewer surprises on payday

HR and finance see the same numbers. Anything that changes pay is approved before the run.

  • Payroll approvals: new joiner salaries, bank changes and expenses in one queue
  • Pre-payroll check: every payslip compared with last month, with the reason for each flag
  • Benefit proofs: flexible benefit declarations collected and verified
  • Statutory deductions and bank files: ready for your payroll run
People records & My Space

One record per person

Each person has one record, with every engagement under it: internship, full-time role, rehire or transfer between your companies.

  • My Space: each employee sees their own record, balances, documents and assets
  • Several companies: one workspace for every legal company in your group
  • Employee ID and work email rules: set once, applied to every new joiner
  • Field history: who changed what and when, on every field
Engagement

A company wall people actually read

Announcements, posts, praise and celebrations in one feed, plus a quick daily mood check-in.

  • Company wall: posts, photos, comments and likes
  • Praise: tied to your company values
  • Celebrations: birthdays and work anniversaries, with opt-out
  • Mood check-in: a two-tap daily pulse, with trends by team
