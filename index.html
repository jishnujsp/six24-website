Why SIX24

An HRMS stores records. SIX24 runs the work around them.

The checks an experienced HR person does by hand every month are built into the product.

Approvals don't get stuck

Leave, WFH and on-duty requests go to the manager first. If nobody acts in two days, the request moves to HR with a flag.

One record per person

Interns who convert, people who come back and transfers between your companies stay on one record, with every change dated.

Payroll checked before payday

Every run is compared with last month. Salary jumps, missing deductions and bank changes are flagged with a reason.
